# Swiggy-Sales-Analysis-Python-PowerBI

# Problem Statement

Swiggy lacks a centralized view of sales performance across states, cities, and time periods, making it difficult to identify high-growth regions, underperforming quarters, and popular food categories. Manual tracking of 20,000+ orders from multiple restaurants leads to missed revenue opportunities and inefficient inventory planning. This project builds an interactive dashboard and Python-based analysis to uncover sales trends, top cities/restaurants, and category-wise revenue contributions for data-driven decision-making.


# Key-takeways

- Total Sales: ₹53M | Total Orders: 197K | Avg Order Value: ₹268 | Avg Rating: 4.3
- Top 5 States by revenue: Karnataka, Uttar Pradesh, Telangana, Maharashtra, Delhi
- Top 5 Cities: Bengaluru, Hyderabad, Ahmedabad, Chennai, Lucknow (New Delhi & Kolkata in top 10)
- Q3 sales dropped 32% vs Q2 (₹19.9M → ₹13.4M) – critical decline
- Weekend revenue (Sat+Sun) is 34% higher than weekdays
- Non-Veg contributes 68% of total revenue (Veg 32%)
- Top restaurants by order volume: McDonald's, KFC, Burger King, Pizza Hut, Domino's
- Best-selling dishes have balanced contribution (16-23% each)
- Low-revenue states: Goa, Meghalaya, Himachal, J&K – expansion opportunity


# Summary (Dashboard and Python Visuals)

- Quarterly Performance: Q1 ₹19.66M (73K orders), Q2 ₹19.90M (74K orders), Q3 ₹13.44M (50K orders)
- Monthly Revenue Trend: Peak mid-year, sharp drop in later months
- Daily Revenue Trend: Highest on Saturday & Sunday
- Revenue by State: Horizontal bar chart – Karnataka highest
- Top 5 Cities by Sales: Bengaluru, Hyderabad, Ahmedabad, Chennai, Lucknow
- Veg vs Non-Veg: Donut chart (68% Non-Veg, 32% Veg)
- Top 5 Restaurants: McDonald's, KFC, Burger King, Pizza Hut, Domino's
- Filters available: Quarter, Month, State, City


# Conclusion

The Swiggy sales analysis shows heavy revenue concentration in top 5 states and cities, with Karnataka leading. Despite a strong average rating of 4.3, the 32% sales decline in Q3 2025 is a major red flag requiring root-cause analysis (competitor offers, delivery issues, or seasonal dip). Non-Veg categories and fast-food chains (McDonald's, KFC) dominate revenue, so menu and partnership focus should remain there. Weekend spikes suggest targeted promotions from Friday to Sunday would maximize ROI. The Power BI dashboard + Python visualizations enable real-time monitoring of state-wise performance, quarterly trends, and restaurant-level strategies.
